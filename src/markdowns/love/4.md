# Learning to love myself

## Long history of self neglect

I know, it does sound bleak that I never really love myself for once. I have no idea why I treated myself like this in the past. But the general feels is that I simply can not imagine how it feels to love myself. While I've crossed path with many great individuals (Eric G., Vallerie, etc.) that inspired me self-love. I could not, for the life of me, learn what it is. I guess it could be my family or friends. Family, especially. Because my parents live a relatively chaotic life style and also lack self-love in general. And in turn, they have never really expressed love to me. I guess it's a chain of <b>loveless</b> history that results to this but alas, the history is long gone, and now I'm more than ready to stay looking forward.

## So how do I learn to self love

A few coincidences made this happen. It did not happen spontaneously but rather a string of events pushed this to happen. First, I've realized whenever I went to some social event, there were people genuinely liked me (a novel concept for me). It seemed to happen more and more recently or I started to pick up on the facts that I was not hated by everyone at least. Second, I decided to quit <b>dating</b> because I felt like I was torturing myself when I couldn't even get comfortable with a stranger (dating is about getting to know a stranger). And by giving it up, I felt a lot of self-care on myself. Combining these 2 factors, I realized I was, indeed, <b>loved</b>. I was too busy self destroying with stressing about getting a successful date all day every day, that I couldn't bring myself to <b>be aware of</b> the realization.

I soon started to have some "me day"s, for example, I went to kareoke alone, it was honestly freeing. I plan to do more "me day"s but for now I'm caught up with life for a bit, and I'll be back to it soon.

Can't wait to plan a bunch of things just for myself, and learn more about caring and loving myself.

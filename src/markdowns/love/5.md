# A friend

![thumbnail](./love_images/5_thumbnail.png)

## Infatuation

A few months ago, I've grown infatuated with a girl from America who's living in China for another few months. I've dived right in, thinking what did I have to lose. When it came to <b>crush</b> moment like this, I often care so little of how reality works, completely contradictory to how I function, which is to think rationally before acting.  

## A trap

What ended up happening was, she was having a partner then, which to me felt not real because how she worded or lied about her relationship as <b>"not sure what she's looking for"</b>. A trap perhaps, because she later continued to have a few more platonic sexual relationships (they last around 1-2 weeks each). Which made me incredibly jealous and, of course, hurt me greatly.

## Friendship

Regardless of how fruitless that "situationship" seemed, it did produce some value for me. Words alone cannot summarize what exactly was the whole encounter. First, We did form a genuine friendship. In fact, it was the <b>strongest friendship</b> I had felt with any female at all. And I could tell it was reciprocated at least in parts by her too. Though I did stubbornly chase her romantically, and she never reciprocated, she continued to ask me to hang out frequently, despite having another new partner during the time being (it was messed up on her part no doubt). And by some degrees, she seemed to at least enjoy parts of it too.

In the end, she flew back to America, saying she's going to come back but I didn't believe her because she had not been consistent with her words since the beginning and lied a ton, including how long had she lived in China, etc.

## The end?

That's it, she messaged me roughly twice after a few months in America then she never replied again. Vallerie, you are a real one. And I'm just an idiot when it comes to love.
